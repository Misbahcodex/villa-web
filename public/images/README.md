# Rizq Luxurious Villa — Photography & Asset Specification

This document provides the complete, authoritative guide for adding real photography to the **Rizq Luxurious Villa** website in **PNG (`.png`) format**, tailored specifically for the **Blossom Luxury Resort & Coliving** editorial design.

---

## 1. Overview & Image Format Guidelines

- **File Format**: Export all edited assets in **PNG (`.png`)** format with high color depth and lossless clarity.
- **Color Profile**: **sRGB**.
- **Transparency**: Architectural and room photos should be solid (opaque) rectangular images. Icons or graphic badges may have transparent backgrounds where noted.
- **Where to save**:
  - Root images go directly to: `public/images/`
  - Room specific images go to: `public/images/rooms/` (or directly in `public/images/`)
  - Amenity images go to: `public/images/amenities/` (or directly in `public/images/`)

---

## 2. Color Palette & Art Direction Guidelines

Your photos should reflect the **Blossom Luxury Boutique Resort** aesthetic: warm, sun-drenched, high-end architectural elegance balanced with digital nomad energy.

### Color Tones to Feature:
| Palette Tone | Hex Reference | Ideal Scene / Subject Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Deep Warm Espresso** | `#1E1A18` | Deep shadows, architectural frames, twilight skies, acoustic dark pod felt |
| **Warm Canvas / Cream** | `#F4F0EC` | Crisp white/cream bed linens, sunlit travertine floors, clean walls |
| **Warm Sand / Chamois** | `#E8DCB8` | Oak tables, natural wood accents, woven loungers, sand-toned textiles |
| **Blossom Coral / Terracotta** | `#F15C3B` | Warm sunset glow, terracotta ceramics, throw cushions, coffee cups |
| **Olive / Lime Green** | `#B5D973` | Courtyard palm leaves, tropical foliage, monstera plants, poolside greenery |
| **Soft Blush Rose** | `#E4C3C3` | Sunrise hues, soft velvet cushions, delicate interior accents |
| **Sunflower Yellow** | `#F6CD48` | Warm interior lights (2700K), afternoon sunlight beams, lemon water garnish |
| **Crystal Azure** | Natural Water | Outdoor swimming pool surface, water caustic reflections against courtyard stone |

### Lighting & Atmosphere:
- **Hero & Outdoors**: Capture during Dubai's golden hour (06:30–08:00 or 17:15–18:30) or blue hour twilight with pool and interior warm LED cove lights glowing.
- **Workspaces & Lounge**: Bright, glare-free, natural daylight streaming through large windows; warm ambient LED pendants.
- **Capsule Pods & Suites**: Calm, cozy, restful illumination; warm reading lights, clean architectural lines, zero clutter.
- **Mood**: Elegant, focused, inviting, and authentic. Real humans (e.g. resident working on a laptop, someone reading poolside) should be natural and candid, never posed stock-photo models.

## 4. Quick Checklist & How to Add Your Photos

1. **Edit & Color Grade**: Apply the warm terracotta, espresso, and canvas palette guidelines above.
2. **Export as PNG**: Save each file with the exact name listed above in lowercase (e.g. `hero-villa.png`, `pool.png`).
3. **Drop Files**:
   - Place general photos in: `c:\Users\hp\Desktop\rizq-website\public\images\`
   - Place room photos in: `c:\Users\hp\Desktop\rizq-website\public\images\rooms\`
4. **Update Metadata / Blur Placeholders**:
   Run the automated audit script in PowerShell:
   ```powershell
   npm run audit:images
   ```
   This script automatically:
   - Scans all `.png` files in `public/images/`.
   - Generates responsive blur-up placeholders.
   - Updates `data/images.generated.json`.
5. **Preview**: Open `http://localhost:3000` in your browser. All placeholders will instantly update to your high-resolution PNG photos!
